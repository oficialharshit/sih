Multi-Stage Forensic AI Architecture

        
Engineered for low latency and high detection accuracy.

      


      

        

          
1. Audio Preprocessing & VAD

          

            Resamples input to 16kHz, performs Voice Activity Detection (VAD) to filter background silence, and divides active speech into uniform temporal chunks.
          

        


        

          
2. Deepfake Voice Classifier

          

            Applies a fine-tuned Wav2Vec2 transformer to inspect raw acoustic spectrograms for unnatural phase artifacts common in synthetic speech synthesis.
          

        


        

          
3. Whisper & DistilBERT NLP

          

            Transcribes spoken audio with Whisper-Small and passes lexical content into a custom DistilBERT classifier trained on social engineering and financial scam prompts.
